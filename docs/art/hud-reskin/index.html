Facet TD · HUD reskin study

Every HUD part, one at a time

The HUD as built today, split into 17 parts. Each part shows its current look on desktop and phone, everything it has to show or do, and its known problems. We pick a part, agree on its new look, and mark it done here.

Captured 30 Oct 2026 from main @ da21e57 · seed 5 · 1440×900 and 390×844 · Chromium

Where each part sits

1 4 2 3 5–9 12 14

Keep step, gem 1 selected. Not visible in this frame: DPS list (13) appears under the menu during waves; tooltip (10) and targeting banner (11) float over the command card; the recipe book (14) opens on the right; dialogs 15–17 cover the board.

Suggested order

  1. KitType scale, colours, panel frame, button and bar styles. Every part reuses these, so we settle them first.
  2. Command card (5–11)The most-used part: one frame with five contents, the tooltip and the targeting banner.
  3. Top bar (2, 3, 12 footer)Round clock, run stats, and castle HP and gold in one status bar that survives every width.
  4. Draft slots (4)The keep decision. Reskin once the card's gem style is set.
  5. Showcase, DPS, recipes (12–14)Side panels that share the kit.
  6. Menus and screens (1, 15–17)Settings, game menu, end card and title.

01

Menu button and settings

top left · Hud.tsx · .tools, #settingsCurrent

Must show

  • Game: Pause P, speed 1× / 2× / 4× 1–3
  • Camera: Top/Isometric T, Drag rotates, Turn left/right, Zoom in/out, Reset view
  • Show: Recipes G, DPS list M, Routes, Auras A, Sound; Hero loadout H (hidden, WIP)
  • Route legend; “New run, restart or exit” Esc

Problems now

P2

Button is 34 px on desktop, 30 px on phone; target is 48.

P3

Toggles and one-shot actions (Turn, Zoom) look the same; on/off reads only by gold fill.

P2

On phone the open panel covers the round clock.

P3

Speed is two clicks away during a wave; it may belong on the top bar.

02

Round clock

top centre · Hud.tsx RoundClock · .round-clockCurrent

Must show

  • Round number (∞N in endless)
  • Run time (small) and round time (large)
  • “Next” or “Left” with creep count; next creep name
  • Paused or Enraged flag

Problems now

P1

Castle HP and gold are not here, and the panels that hold them vanish at ≤900 px. The top bar is the natural home for both.

P3

Two unlabelled clocks; labels at 10 px. Creep name repeats in the showcase.

03

Run stats

top right · Hud.tsx StatsPanel · .stats-panelCurrent

Must show

  • Mode (Classic / Facet)
  • Kills / leaks, maze length, creeps on board, wave progress %

Problems now

P2

No panel behind it; sits on the cave scenery. Labels 10 px.

P3

“K/M” and “Length” explained only in hover tooltips.

04

Draft slots

top left, build only · Hud.tsx DraftSlots · .draftCurrent

Must show

  • Title: “Place gems (n/5)”, then “Keep one gem or combine”
  • Per slot: number, gem model icon, code (P1, Y1…), type colour, “can combine” dot, selected state
  • Empty slots disabled; clicking a filled slot selects its gem on the board

Problems now

P2

Slots 36 px wide on phone; slot numbers 9 px.

P3

Empty slots barely visible; no hint of how to place the next gem.

05

Command card: builder

bottom centre, nothing selected · CommandCard.tsx HeroCard · .cardCurrent

The frame (shared by 05–09)

  • Portrait: name, art, level disc, four stats down the right edge
  • Status effects row; one line of commands with hotkey above, icon, label, cooldown, primary/glow/disabled states
  • Two bars (health-style green, mana-style dark) and a note line for reasons and refusals
  • Close Esc; collapse on tablet and phone

Builder content

  • Build level, gold, towers, stones
  • Place gem Q, Remove stone W; hero skills EDFR (WIP)
  • Bars: castle HP, gems placed

Problems now

P2

760 px wide with two commands; bars unlabelled; stats are icons at 9–10 px, bare numbers on phone.

P2

Phone shows Q/W hints and cuts names to “Place …”, “Remo…”.

06

Command card: gem being placed

a draft gem selected · CommandCard.tsx CellCardCurrent

Must show

  • Gem name, type and quality; damage, attack speed, range, kills
  • Ability (e.g. Split, Shred) as a passive slot
  • Keep K, Keep +1, Keep +2, Combine C (when possible), Hold fire S
  • Bars: share of wave damage, MVP progress; note: why an action is refused

Problems now

P2

Name truncated (“CHIPPED AMETHYS…”) at the keep decision.

P2

The label on every gold primary button (“Keep”, “Place gem”) is dark-on-dark and unreadable.

Problems now

P2
