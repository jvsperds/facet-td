# Effect-driven tower identities

This finalized visual catalogue uses the baseline tower descriptions and assigned abilities, rather than gem names alone. The audit checked English tower help, ability descriptions and relevant modifier definitions on phase-1/foundation in data/baseline/localization/english.json, npc/units.json and npc/abilities.json.

The source-role summary and design cues appear beneath the study when a combined tower or family is selected. combined-effect-notes.js records each source help description and assigned ability IDs. These are visual motifs; they do not implement combat effects or change gameplay data.

Important distinctions: Yellow Sapphire is explicitly an ice slow aura and its original effect uses snowflakes; Gold primarily strips armour, with greed added by Egypt Gold; Pearl protects nearby towers from magic rather than silencing enemies; Natural Zumurud steals three abilities despite having empty assigned ability slots, so its role comes from its tower help text.
